Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Meridian Studio is a one-person web design studio based in New South Wales, Australia. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the details you send me.

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your business name (if you add it), your email address, and your message. Nothing else is collected, and there are no tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

Why I collect it

So I can reply to your enquiry and talk with you about your project. That's the only reason.

How it's handled

  • Form messages are delivered to my email through Formspree, a form-handling service. Your message passes through their systems to reach my inbox.
  • I don't sell or share your details, and I won't add you to any mailing list.
  • I keep enquiries only as long as needed to help you, then delete them on request.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to show you what I hold, correct it, or delete it. Just email officialmeridianstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date above changes with it.
