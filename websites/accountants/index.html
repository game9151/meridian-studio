Built for how accountants get work.

Most people pick an accountant in a hurry, close to a deadline, after reading two or three websites. These are the parts that make yours the one they book.

A fee guide that does the maths

Clients tick what applies (wage, rental property, ABN, shares) and see the service and the fee. You stop quoting individual returns by email.

What-to-bring checklists

A printable list for each kind of return: income statements, rental statements, a car logbook. Clients arrive with the right paperwork and appointments run to time.

Key dates with a calendar button

Quarterly BAS dates and the 31 October deadline for people lodging their own return, each with add-to-calendar. It brings clients back every quarter without a newsletter.

Booking that suits tax time

Online booking with 30 and 60 minute slots, extra days open from July, and a phone option for the clients who will never book online.

A clear link to the client portal you already use, so nobody emails a tax file number. If you don't have one, I'll tell you the simplest option.

Questions accountants ask.

Something else? Ask it in the form below.

Do I have to show prices?

No, but it helps. A "from" price is enough. It puts off the people who were only ever going to choose the cheapest, and it saves you a lot of quoting.

Can clients upload their documents through the site?

For tax file numbers and bank details, they should use your client portal, and the site links to it. A simple upload form that emails you is possible for everything else.

Can you have it live before tax time?

Yes, if we start by [MONTH]. Tell me your busy dates and I'll plan around them.

Can you do separate pages for individuals and businesses?

Yes. Individuals usually want a price and a booking slot. Businesses want to know you handle BAS, payroll and their software. They get different pages.

Will it work with my practice software?

The site links to your portal and booking tool rather than replacing them, so nothing in your practice changes.

Do you write the words?

Yes. I draft them from your fee list and a short call, and you edit anything that doesn't sound like you.
