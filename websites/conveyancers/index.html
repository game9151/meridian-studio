Built for how conveyancers get work.

Buyers usually compare a few conveyancers on price, then pick the one who seems least likely to drop the ball. These are the parts of the site that win that comparison.

A fee calculator

Buying or selling, house or unit, price band. It shows your fixed fee and a realistic range for searches and disbursements, so the comparison happens on your page instead of over the phone.

Send-a-contract upload

Agents send the contract the day an offer goes in. A button that takes the PDF and lands it in your inbox gets you the review while the buyer is still deciding who to use.

Cooling-off and settlement dates

A small tool that counts business days from exchange answers the question clients ring about most. You set the rules and check the dates before launch.

Licence and cover where people look

Your NSW Fair Trading licence number, PEXA membership and professional indemnity cover sit in the footer and on the about page. The trust questions are answered without a call.

The process in plain English

First-home buyers don't know what a section 10.7 certificate or a pest and building report is. A step-by-step page in their words cuts down the "what happens next" emails.

Pages for your suburbs and agents

Optional pages for the suburbs you settle in most, and a referral page for local agents, so you can turn up for searches like "conveyancer Strathfield".

Questions conveyancers ask.

Something else? Ask it in the form below.

Can it show different fees for buying and selling?

Yes. The calculator works from your own table, so buying, selling, off-the-plan and strata can each have their own fee and disbursement range.

Where do uploaded contracts go?

Straight to your email as an attachment. The website doesn't keep a copy.

I'm a solicitor, not a licensed conveyancer. Does that change anything?

A little. Solicitors have stricter advertising rules, so I leave out client reviews and add the wording your practice needs. You confirm it before launch.

Do you write the words?

Yes. I write the first draft from your fee schedule and a short call, and you change anything that doesn't sound like you.

Will my email stop working if I move my website?

No. Only the website records change. Your email stays exactly where it is.

What if I already have a site?

I can rebuild it at the same address. Your old page addresses get redirected so you don't lose what Google already knows about you.
